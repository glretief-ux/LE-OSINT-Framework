About this framework

    
A directory of open-source intelligence resources organised for law enforcement investigators, organised by line of enquiry rather than as one alphabetical tree. Besides the classic OSINT categories it adds categories for maritime and container intelligence, customs and trade, drugs, sanctions, wanted persons, financial crime, platform law-enforcement portals, legal frameworks and international cooperation.

    
How to use it

    
    
      
  1. Type or paste a selector in the search bar. The type (email, container number, wallet…) is detected automatically; click the type chip to change it.
    2. 
      
  2. A run sheet appears: every source that accepts that selector, already filled in, grouped by line of enquiry. Opening a search ticks it off; the progress bar shows what is left.
    3. 
      
  3. Click Log next to a search to add it to the case log with a UTC timestamp. Export the log from the Toolbox.
    4. 
      
  4. With the search bar empty, the left rail lets you browse all sources by line of enquiry.

Operational security

    
    
      
  • Work from an approved, non-attributable research environment. Never use personal accounts.
    • 
      
  • Search a file's hash before you upload the file anywhere. Public sandboxes share uploads.
    • 
      
  • Do not paste case material into public translation or AI services unless your agency has approved them.
    • 
      
  • Collect only what is necessary and proportionate. Your authority, not the tool's availability, decides what you may do.
    • 
    

    
Privacy of this page

    
This page makes no network requests with your selector. Searches go directly from your browser to the site you click. The Toolbox runs entirely in your browser; the case log is kept in this browser's local storage until you clear it.
