# Contributing

Suggestions from investigators, analysts and trainers are welcome.

## Suggest a resource
Open an issue with: site name, URL, category, whether it needs login or payment, and (if it has a search box) an example search URL.

## Inclusion criteria
- Useful for a lawful investigation, and still maintained.
- Publicly reachable, or clearly flagged `L` if it is for law enforcement or government accounts only.
- No sites that sell stolen data, facilitate crime, or host abuse material.
- Flag anything that may alert the subject (`A`) or carries privacy or legal risk (`P`).

## Editing
All links are in `data/links.js`. Keep one link per line: `Name | URL | FLAGS | SELECTORS`.
